Hello, I’m Jared.

I care about the story and how people experience it.

For more than 15 years, I’ve worked in higher education creating visual stories and shaping digital experiences. I started behind the camera. Today, I bring that same attention to detail to managing websites, supporting editors, and making information easier to find and use.

My favorite projects connect the creative and the practical: a strong image, a clear message, and a page that actually works for the people who need it.
